# Related Work

## Language models of audio

AudioLM (Borsos et al., 2023) showed that audio can be generated by
language-modelling discrete tokens, using a hierarchy of semantic and
acoustic tokens. MusicLM (Agostinelli et al., 2023) conditioned this approach
on text through a joint music-text embedding. MusicGen (Copet et al., 2023)
simplified the hierarchy to a single Transformer over the codebooks of a
neural codec, interleaved by a *delay pattern* so that all codebooks of a
frame are predicted in one pass. StaleyNET adopts MusicGen's delay pattern,
top-k sampling and classifier-free guidance, but replaces the pretrained
codec with a k-means quantiser over log-mel features and the pretrained text
encoder with raw UTF-8 bytes, an approach studied for text by ByT5 (Xue et
al., 2022).

## Singing and lyrics

Jukebox (Dhariwal et al., 2020) generated singing conditioned on artist,
genre and lyrics, using lyrics-to-audio attention trained on 1.2 million
songs. Singing voice synthesis systems such as DiffSinger (Liu et al., 2022)
instead condition on a musical score with phoneme durations, which gives
precise control but requires annotated scores. StaleyNET follows the Jukebox
direction at a much smaller scale: it conditions on lyrics as text and learns
the alignment from data. The training signal for the alignment comes from
JamendoLyrics (Stoller, Durand and Ewert, 2019; Durand, Stoller and Ewert,
2023), a lyrics-alignment benchmark whose line-level time stamps allow each
training excerpt to be paired with the words sung in it.

## Audio representations

Neural codecs (SoundStream, Zeghidour et al., 2021; EnCodec, Défossez et
al., 2022; DAC, Kumar et al., 2023) learn an encoder, a residual vector
quantiser and a decoder jointly, and reach high fidelity at a few kilobits
per second. They require large training sets and substantial compute.
StaleyNET retains the residual quantisation principle but applies it, by
k-means, directly to normalised log-mel frames, so that the tokenizer can be
trained in minutes on an edge device. Waveforms are recovered with the
Griffin-Lim algorithm (Griffin and Lim, 1984) in its accelerated form
(Perraudin, Balazs and Søndergaard, 2013), rather than with a neural vocoder
such as HiFi-GAN (Kong, Kim and Bae, 2020) or Vocos (Siuzdak, 2023). The
consequences of both choices are quantified in
[Experiments](experiments.md#5-the-audio-pipeline-in-isolation).

## Efficient Transformer computation

Key/value caching for autoregressive inference is standard practice (Pope et
al., 2023). Register-blocked matrix multiplication on GPUs was analysed by
Volkov and Demmel (2008); FlashAttention (Dao et al., 2022) fuses attention to
avoid materialising the score matrix. StaleyNET implements the first two in
Rust through cuda-oxide and leaves fused attention to future work.
